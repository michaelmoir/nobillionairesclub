# Episode 004 - AI Promises, Public Costs

A practical discussion on who captures upside from AI adoption and who absorbs the downside.

## Topics covered

- Investment narratives versus real-world deployment outcomes
- Hidden public costs: energy use, data extraction, and labor externalities
- What public-interest AI policy should prioritize

## Questions for listeners

- Where do you see AI improving work, and where does it shift risk onto workers?
- Which AI claims deserve stronger evidence before policy or procurement decisions?

## Further reading ideas

- Public procurement standards for AI tools
- Worker impact assessments and algorithmic accountability
