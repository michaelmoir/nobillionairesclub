# Episode 003 - Manufactured Outrage and the 99%

How outrage-driven media cycles can fracture solidarity while leaving economic power untouched.

## Topics covered

- Why conflict-heavy coverage is rewarded in modern media systems
- How symbolic fights can distract from wages, housing, healthcare, and labor rights
- Practical ways to bring conversations back to material conditions

## Questions for listeners

- Which issues in your area are framed as "culture war" but are really economic?
- What local stories are missing from national media narratives?

## Further reading ideas

- Media incentives and platform amplification
- Labor reporting and union campaign coverage
